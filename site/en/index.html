Just speak.It’s written.

HibiVo is a voice input app for Mac. Press a key and speak, and clean text appears at the cursor in whatever app you’re using. It’s built Japanese-first, and handles English too. It can also transcribe meetings and write the minutes.

Switches automatically by app

Business in Mail, Prompt in Terminal. You choose which mode each app uses.

AI cleanup needs an API key

Choose from Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, Google Gemini, or an OpenAI-compatible API. You pay the service you use. If cleanup fails, what you said is entered as is.

Fix a word once, and the dictionary learns it

When you correct a word right after dictating, HibiVo notices and adds it to your dictionary, so company names, product names and technical terms come out right more often. Click the notice to undo. In apps whose text can’t be read, such as Terminal, you can add words from History.

Meetings, written down

Hold Right Option and press M to start. HibiVo transcribes your microphone and system audio together, so remote participants are captured too.

  • On macOS 26 or later, built-in transcription needs no API key and costs nothing. To tell speakers apart, use Soniox.
  • HibiVo can also write minutes (summary, decisions, to-dos and more) with your AI cleanup provider or Claude Code. This is off by default.
  • Where recording or transcription requires consent, tell participants first.

You choose what goes where

After transcription, no audio stays on your Mac
Dictation and real-time meetings use audio in memory and never write it to disk. Only After the Meeting transcription stores it on your Mac until the transcript is done, then deletes it. If transcription can’t finish, it’s deleted after 7 days.
With macOS transcription, audio stays on your Mac
That’s the default on macOS 26 or later. If you choose Soniox or Gemini, audio goes to that service. If you use AI cleanup, text goes to the provider you chose.
API keys live in the Keychain
They’re never written to a settings file. History is kept on your Mac in plain text, up to 200 entries, and you can turn it off.
No analytics or telemetry
The app sends no usage data, and this page has no analytics. It does check GitHub for updates, which you can turn off.

Get started

  1. Download

    Get the zip from GitHub Releases, unzip it, and move HibiVo.app to Applications.

  2. Allow two permissions

    Accessibility, for the hotkey and pasting, and the microphone.

  3. Press right Option and speak

    Press again when you’re done, or hold while you speak. You can also use Fn, right Command, or ⌃Space.
